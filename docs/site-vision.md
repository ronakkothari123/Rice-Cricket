# Site Vision

## Vision Summary

The Rice Cricket Club website should function as both a recruiting tool and a credibility platform.

For students, it should make the club feel active, welcoming, and exciting to join. For administrators, alumni, and other stakeholders, it should communicate continuity, visible momentum, and the long-term value of supporting cricket on campus. The site should feel like a modern collegiate sports presence with editorial polish: energetic without chaos, warm without looking casual, and professional without becoming stiff.

Version 1 should prove that Rice Cricket is not just a niche interest group, but a growing student organization with real community, real activity, and a real future.

## What The Website Needs To Accomplish

- Turn curiosity into action from incoming and current students
- Show evidence that the club is active and growing
- Capture the atmosphere of the club through strong imagery and storytelling
- Establish a trustworthy public face that feels durable beyond one officer cycle
- Quietly reinforce the case for better cricket infrastructure at Rice

## Core Strategic Messages

Every major page should reinforce some combination of these messages:

- Rice Cricket is active, not hypothetical
- New members are welcome, including beginners
- The club offers both competition and community
- The organization has continuity, leadership, and ambition
- Support for the club benefits student life and campus culture

## Audience Breakdown

| Audience | What They Care About | What They Need To See | Desired Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Incoming students | Finding community quickly, whether they can belong, whether the club is beginner-friendly | Photos of real students, welcoming language, clear join steps, what participation looks like in the first few weeks | Reach out by email, follow the club, attend a first practice or info event |
| Prospective students | Whether Rice supports diverse interests, whether campus life feels dynamic, whether there is room for global sports culture | A polished homepage, evidence of activity, club story, visual energy, match and event history | Leave with a strong impression that Rice has a vibrant and inclusive student culture |
| Current Rice students | Time commitment, skill level expectations, social atmosphere, whether joining is still realistic mid-year | Join page clarity, beginner-to-experienced pathways, season rhythm, recent events, welcoming team culture | Contact the club and show up to a practice, match, or social event |
| Rice administrators and stakeholders | Student impact, continuity, legitimacy, utilization, evidence of need, responsible leadership | Leadership structure, milestones, match history, evidence of growth, facilities vision, clear organizational narrative | View the club as a credible organization worth supporting through space, recognition, or future facilities investment |
| Alumni, supporters, and friends | Pride, progress, continuity, ways to stay connected, whether the club is building something lasting | Stories, milestones, leadership continuity, gallery highlights, future vision | Stay connected, share the site, advise, donate in the future, or advocate informally |

## Audience Priorities By Page

- `Home` should win attention from all audiences and route them quickly.
- `Join` should remove uncertainty for students.
- `Gallery` should carry the emotional recruiting burden.
- `Stories` and `Matches` should prove that the club is active.
- `Vision` should help stakeholders understand why future facilities support matters.

## Experience Qualities

The site should feel:

- Welcoming
- Organized
- Energetic
- Premium
- Collegiate
- Ambitious

It should not feel:

- Generic
- Temporary
- Amateurish
- Overdesigned
- Loud in the style of a professional sports scoreboard site

## Success Criteria For Version 1

The website is doing its job if it can:

- Convince a new student that joining is realistic and appealing
- Show a parent, administrator, or advisor that the club is legitimate and active
- Give supporters confidence that the club has momentum and continuity
- Provide a clean content structure that future officers can update without rethinking the entire site

## Strategic Positioning

Rice Cricket sits at an intersection that the website should embrace:

- A student organization, not a varsity team
- A competitive sport, not just a social club
- A culturally meaningful activity with broad appeal
- A growing campus initiative that can make a serious case for more support over time

The best version of the site will make those identities feel coherent rather than conflicted.
