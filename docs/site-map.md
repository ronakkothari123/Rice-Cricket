# Site Map And Information Architecture

## Recommended Top-Level Site Map

```text
Home
About
Join
Leadership
Matches
Stories
Gallery
Vision
```

## Why These Pages

- `Home` is the routing and first-impression layer.
- `About` gives the club a stable institutional and cultural narrative.
- `Join` lowers friction for students who are interested but unsure.
- `Leadership` signals continuity and accountability without creating a maintenance-heavy full roster.
- `Matches` proves competitive activity and creates a useful archive.
- `Stories` centralizes updates, recaps, milestones, and features in one editorial system.
- `Gallery` sells culture, energy, and belonging more effectively than text alone.
- `Vision` provides a credible long-term case for campus support and future facilities.

## Naming Decisions

- Use `Leadership` instead of `Team` or `People` for version 1 because it stays accurate even if a public full roster is not actively maintained.
- Use `Stories` instead of `News` or `Blog` because it feels broader, more editorial, and less dependent on high publishing frequency.
- Keep `Contact` out of the primary navigation and surface it through `Join`, homepage CTAs, header utility space, and the footer.

## Primary Navigation Strategy

- Logo links to `Home`
- Main nav items: `About`, `Join`, `Leadership`, `Matches`, `Stories`, `Gallery`, `Vision`
- `Join` should be visually emphasized as the primary action
- A compact utility treatment can expose the club email and social links without turning the header into a directory

## Footer Strategy

The footer should group links into:

- Explore
- Join
- Contact
- Follow
- Club Snapshot

This helps stakeholders and alumni find context quickly without bloating the main navigation.

## Homepage CTA Paths

- Primary CTA: `Join Rice Cricket`
- Secondary CTA: `See the Club in Action`
- Tertiary CTA: `Explore the Vision`

These three paths map to the site's main jobs: recruit, prove activity, and build institutional credibility.

## User Flow Guidance

- Students should be pulled toward `Join`, `Gallery`, and recent stories or events.
- Stakeholders should be guided from `Home` into `About`, then toward `Matches`, `Stories`, and `Vision`.
- Alumni and supporters should quickly find progress, continuity, and future ambitions.

## Page-By-Page Section Breakdown

### Home

| Section | Purpose | Content | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hero | Create an immediate emotional and visual hook | Strong headline, supporting copy, primary and secondary CTAs, striking action or team image | Sets the tone and quickly establishes that the club is active and real |
| Proof strip | Deliver fast evidence of momentum | Club age or founding year, number of seasons, milestone highlights, match or event counts | Gives administrators and students immediate proof that the club has substance |
| Club snapshot | Explain what Rice Cricket is in one scan | Short mission statement, who can join, what the club offers | Prevents confusion for visitors unfamiliar with cricket or club sports |
| Culture preview | Make the club feel welcoming and alive | Short copy with candid images of practices, matches, and community moments | Helps newcomers picture themselves in the club |
| Recent activity | Show current momentum | Latest story cards, featured match, or milestone updates | Signals that the club is active in the present, not just historically |
| Vision teaser | Introduce long-term ambition | Short section on facilities and future growth with a link to the full vision page | Connects current activity to long-term campus value |
| Contact CTA | Create a clean next step | Email-first action, optional social links, reassurance for beginners | Converts interest into outreach |

### About

| Section | Purpose | Content | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Intro | State the club's mission and identity | Short overview of who the club is and why it exists | Gives the organization a clear public voice |
| Why cricket at Rice | Explain relevance on campus | Framing around diversity, community, competition, and student life | Helps outsiders understand why the club matters |
| Timeline | Show continuity and growth | Founding, relaunches, milestones, tournaments, partnerships, recognition | Institutional proof matters for credibility |
| What the club offers | Clarify member experience | Practice, matches, socials, leadership, community | Helps students understand the value proposition |
| Values and culture | Set expectations | Welcoming tone, beginner-friendly stance, competitive spirit, sportsmanship | Attracts the right members and signals maturity |
| FAQ | Answer common questions | Experience level, equipment, scheduling, time commitment | Reduces repetitive outreach and hesitation |

### Join

| Section | Purpose | Content | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Join hero | Make the invitation explicit | Message that players of all experience levels are welcome | Recruitment should be unambiguous |
| Who should join | Expand the funnel | Beginners, experienced players, casual participants, supporters | Broadens perceived fit |
| Pathways | Address different skill levels | Beginner, returning, and experienced-player pathways | Reduces the fear of not being "good enough" |
| Season rhythm | Explain commitment | Practices, matches, socials, peak periods, expected flexibility | Helps current students judge feasibility |
| First contact expectations | Demystify outreach | What happens after someone emails, what to bring, what first attendance is like | Lowers anxiety and increases conversion |
| Contact block | Provide the main action | Club email, optional social links, officer contact guidance | Keeps the join flow simple for version 1 |
| FAQ | Remove final objections | Equipment, attendance expectations, fees if any, experience questions | Prevents drop-off at the decision stage |

### Leadership

| Section | Purpose | Content | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Leadership intro | Frame the page | Explanation of officer roles and continuity | Signals responsible organization |
| Officer grid | Humanize the club | Current officers with photos, roles, bios, academic info, and contact points | Helps students and stakeholders see real leadership |
| Responsibilities | Explain how the club runs | Who handles recruitment, scheduling, logistics, outreach | Builds trust with administrators and prospective members |
| Community spotlights | Show culture beyond titles | Member spotlights, captains, volunteers, or standout contributors | Makes the page feel more human without requiring a full roster |
| Support and continuity | Show durability | Optional alumni mention, advisor mention, handoff culture | Reinforces long-term legitimacy |

### Matches

| Section | Purpose | Content | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Season overview | Frame the current competitive story | Current season summary, competitions, priorities, goals | Gives the page context beyond isolated results |
| Fixtures and results | Present the core archive | Match cards or table grouped by season or status | Demonstrates consistent activity |
| Featured result | Highlight a proof point | Best win, close match, rivalry, milestone performance | Adds narrative energy to the archive |
| Recap links | Connect results to storytelling | Links into stories or recap pages | Makes the competitive record feel alive |
| Archives | Preserve history | Prior seasons with a clean, scannable structure | Useful for continuity and stakeholder proof |

### Stories

| Section | Purpose | Content | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stories hero | Establish the editorial layer | Intro text and optional featured story | Frames this as the club's living narrative |
| Featured story | Lead with the strongest current story | Highlighted recap, milestone, or feature | Gives the section an editorial center |
| Story grid | Organize updates | Cards for announcements, recaps, profiles, milestones, and features | Supports easy browsing and future growth |
| Topic tags | Aid discovery | Category tags such as recap, milestone, feature, announcement | Keeps one collection flexible without becoming messy |
| Milestone highlights | Surface credibility moments | Founding milestones, tournament entries, campus recognition | Helps stakeholders quickly find meaningful proof |

### Gallery

| Section | Purpose | Content | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gallery intro | Set context for the visuals | Brief framing around practices, matches, socials, and travel if relevant | Gives images narrative context |
| Featured albums | Showcase the strongest image sets | Key events, tournaments, team portraits, campus practice sessions | Helps the best visuals lead the experience |
| Gallery grid | Provide broad browsing | Cover thumbnails with dates, tags, and summaries | Lets visitors explore the club's atmosphere quickly |
| Lightbox experience | Encourage deeper immersion | Large images, captions, keyboard navigation, reduced-motion-aware interaction | Makes photography feel premium and intentional |
| Captions and context | Add story to visuals | Opponents, event names, season notes, celebration moments | Prevents the gallery from becoming just a dump of photos |

### Vision

| Section | Purpose | Content | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Vision intro | Frame the long-term case | Statement about growth, campus value, and facilities ambition | Makes the page feel thoughtful rather than needy |
| Why facilities matter | Explain the problem clearly | Limits of current setup, training constraints, match hosting limitations | Gives stakeholders a practical reason to care |
| Growth evidence | Prove demand | Membership growth, event history, interest levels, match activity | Support requests need evidence |
| Campus value | Tie the club to Rice's mission | Community, cultural reach, student wellness, leadership opportunities | Makes support feel institutionally aligned |
| Milestones already achieved | Show seriousness | What the club has already built with current resources | Strengthens the case for future investment |
| Future goals | State the next horizon | Nets, pitch access, improved scheduling, event hosting potential | Turns ambition into a credible roadmap |
